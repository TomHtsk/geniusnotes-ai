✦ Key Points
Memory is divided into three stages: encoding, storage, and retrieval. Failure at any stage causes forgetting.
The hippocampus plays a central role in converting short-term memories into long-term ones — damage to it causes anterograde amnesia.
Spaced repetition increases long-term retention by up to 200% compared to massed practice (cramming).
Sleep consolidates memories — students who sleep after studying retain 40% more than those who stay awake.
Takeaway
Understanding how memory works lets you study smarter — use spaced repetition, sleep well, and retrieve actively rather than re-reading passively.

Homework Solver — Algebra
❓ Solve: 2x² + 5x − 3 = 0
Solution
Step 1 Identify coefficients: a = 2, b = 5, c = −3
Step 2 Apply the quadratic formula: x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / 2a
Step 3 Discriminant: b²−4ac = 25 + 24 = 49
Step 4 x = (−5 ± 7) / 4 → x = 0.5 or x = −3
✓ Two real solutions: x = ½ and x = −3

Magic Highlighter — "Key terms"

Chapter 4: The Cell

All living things are made of cells, the basic unit of life. Cells are surrounded by a cell membrane that controls what enters and exits. Inside, the nucleus contains DNA and directs cell activity.

Mitosis is the process by which a cell divides to produce two identical daughter cells. This is distinct from meiosis, which produces four genetically unique cells for reproduction.

The chloroplast — found only in plant cells — uses sunlight to produce glucose via photosynthesis.
