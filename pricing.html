Questions

What is an AI credit?

Each AI tool uses credits. Short tasks like Define, Grammar, Translate or the tutor chat cost 1 credit. Bigger tasks, such as summaries, study guides, flashcards, Create picture or reading scanned pages, cost 1 credit for every 5,000 characters (or 2 pages), up to 5 credits. If something fails, the credits are given back.

What counts as transcription?

Record Lecture and turning uploaded audio or video into text. Transcription uses your monthly hours, never your AI credits. The YouTube converter is separate and free for everyone (3 a day).

When do credits and hours reset?

On the 1st of every month. Unused credits and hours don't carry over.

What happens if I run out?

That tool pauses until the 1st of next month, or you can upgrade. You are never charged extra automatically — the only charge is the plan you chose.

Can I cancel or switch plans?

Yes, any time, from "Manage subscription" on this page. If you cancel, you keep your plan until the end of the period you already paid for, then move to Free.

Payments are in test mode — no real money is charged.
