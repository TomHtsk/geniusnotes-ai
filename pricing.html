Questions

What is an AI credit?

Each AI tool uses credits, based on how much it reads: 1 credit for every 5,000 characters of text (a short task is 1 credit), and 1 credit for every 2 scanned pages or photos. One request can be up to 50,000 characters or 20 pages. Anything costing 2 or more credits shows the cost and asks first. If something fails, the credits are given back.

What counts as transcription?

Record Lecture and turning uploaded audio or video into text. It uses your transcription time, never your AI credits. Plain transcripts from the YouTube converter are free for everyone (3 a day); an AI summary of a video uses credits.

When do credits and hours refill?

Paid plans refill every month on your billing date, once that month's payment goes through (yearly plans refill monthly too). Free refills on the 1st of each month. Unused monthly credits and hours don't carry over.

What happens if I run out?

Your notes and saved work stay available and tools that don't use AI keep working. You can wait for your refill, upgrade, or (on Student and Pro) buy a top-up: 100 AI credits for $4.99 or 2 hours of transcription for $2.99. A top-up is only bought when you choose it and confirm the price.

What counts as cloud storage?

Your notes (including pictures and photos inside them), folders, history, flashcard decks, notebooks and shared notes. Free includes 100 MB, Student 2 GB and Pro 10 GB. If you go over, nothing is deleted: you can still open, edit, shorten and delete notes, and new changes are saved on your device, but they only sync to the cloud once you are back under the limit or upgrade.

What if a payment fails?

You keep using what's left of your allowance while the card is retried, but there's no refill until a payment goes through. If it never does, your plan ends and you move to Free.

How does the free month with a code work?

With a launch offer code, the first 100 new members get their first 30 days of Student free. You add a card at checkout and pay $0 that day. If you don't cancel before the free month ends, your card is charged for Student ($4.99 a month, or $49.90 a year if you chose yearly) from that day. To avoid any charge, cancel before the end date under "Manage subscription" on this page; you keep Student until the free month ends. One free month per account.

Can I cancel or switch plans?

Yes, any time, from "Manage subscription" on this page. Upgrading from Student to Pro charges the difference for the rest of this period right away and raises your allowance at once. Moving down to Student, or from yearly to monthly, starts at your next renewal. If you cancel, you keep your plan until the end of the period you already paid for, then move to Free.

Payments are in test mode — no real money is charged.
